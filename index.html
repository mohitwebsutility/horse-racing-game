How to play

W/↑Run (auto-run is on by default)
S/↓Brake
AD / ←→Steer left / right (drift back to lane centre when released)
SpaceStick hit – one hit per press, subject to cooldown and stamina. Tap in a steady rhythm; spamming drains stamina.
RRear (penalty: the horse stops briefly; 8 s cooldown)
VChange camera · drag to orbit · wheel to zoom
TToggle reins
EnterStart race
EscPause / resume

Touch devices get on-screen buttons automatically. Win by crossing the finish line first — results use actual line-crossing times.
